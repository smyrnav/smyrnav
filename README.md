## Interests

I'm interested in industrial ecology, circular economy and resource efficiency, particularly the way raw materials, food and other physical resources move through economic systems and investigate the solutions to the supply-demand challenges in the modern business operations. I want my contribution to result in healthier business models and more ethical practices that not only prevent the outcomes mentioned above, but actively help mitigate and reverse the damage that already has been done.
